ICRA 2026 · ARXIV 2602.01189

SPOT: Spatio-Temporal Obstacle-free Trajectory Planning

A mapless planner for quadrotors in unknown, changing environments. It builds safe flight corridors directly from vision, separates static from moving obstacles, and falls back to a reactive backup plan when no direct route exists. Validated in simulation and on real hardware.

A. Srivastava, T. J. Chackenkulam, B. Bhanu Teja, A. Thomas, K. M. Krishna
arXiv ↗PDF ↗
ICCA 2025 · IEEE

DISCO: Diffusion-Based Inter-Agent Swarm Collision-Free Optimization

Multi-agent trajectory planning for UAV swarms in confined spaces. A diffusion model proposes a diverse set of candidate trajectories, and an optimisation layer filters them for safety, so the swarm can adapt to different constraints instead of committing to one plan.

Co-authored by B. Bhanu Teja
